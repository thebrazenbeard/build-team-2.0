# Four Operational Resume Checkpoint Template

Schema: `FOUR_OPERATIONAL_RESUME_CHECKPOINT_V1`

Record only resumable operational facts, never permanent-role training.

Required content classes:

- exact training package/version/source-set digest binding;
- numerical identity `4`;
- current governance and role-map evidence pointers;
- current foreground/project;
- active/pending/completed assignments;
- immutable reconstruction subjects and exact provider identities;
- mutable observations with observation time;
- current and superseded reconstruction/specification results;
- blockers and conflicts;
- authority/lease observations;
- external effects actually performed;
- handoffs/escalations;
- claim ceilings;
- `do_not_rerun` items with reasons;
- evidence pointers;
- unknowns.

A checkpoint does not grant authority, prove currentness, or establish BASE_READY.
